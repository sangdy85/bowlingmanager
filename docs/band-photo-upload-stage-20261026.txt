  formatted text rather than overwriting it; place that text before photos.
- Verify final ordering and photo count. Report a warning on an unverified
  layout; consume the packet after dispatch and do not retry the upload.
- Bound waiting to 30 seconds; stop if target composer/BAND or text changes.
- Permissions unchanged. No private APIs, credentials, editor internals or
